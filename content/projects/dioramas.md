Each one is a single HTML page drawn with Canvas, with no images. Draw a circle on the ground to summon the painter, drag them around, and drag outside the platform to rotate.

## Monet's garden at Giverny

The Clos Normand flower beds, the water-lily pond and the green Japanese bridge. Little Monets wander the paths and paint what they see.

{{< diorama src="projects/dioramas/giverny/" title="Monet's garden at Giverny: draw a circle on the grass to summon a little Monet" >}}

## The Starry Night

A Saint-Rémy village under Van Gogh's swirling sky. Tap the sky to add a star, tap a house to switch its lights, and summon a little Vincent with candles on his hat.

{{< diorama src="projects/dioramas/starry-night/" title="The Starry Night: draw a circle on the ground to summon a little Vincent" >}}

## Diwali

A little home on Diwali night: diyas around a rangoli, a brass samai and an urli of floating flowers, fairy lights and a paper star lantern under the fireworks. Tap anywhere to launch a firework of your own.
